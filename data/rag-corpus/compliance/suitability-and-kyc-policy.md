# Suitability & KYC Policy (Retail Advisory)

**Compliance manual chapter 3, revision 2026-02. Mandatory for all relationship managers.**

## Risk-profile ladder

Every advised household carries exactly one of five risk profiles, refreshed at least every 24 months or on any material life event:

| Profile | Target growth assets | Max single stock | Crypto eligible | Typical drawdown tolerance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Conservative | 20–35% | 5% | No | -10% |
| Moderately Conservative | 35–50% | 7% | No | -15% |
| Moderate | 50–65% | 10% | No | -20% |
| Moderately Aggressive | 65–80% | 10% | Yes, ≤2% | -30% |
| Aggressive | 80–100% | 10% | Yes, ≤5% | -40%+ |

Allocation drift beyond ±10 percentage points from the profile target requires a rebalancing conversation at the next client contact and must be documented even if the client declines to act.

## Know-your-client refresh triggers

Re-run the KYC questionnaire when any of these occur: liquidity event (business sale, inheritance, exercised equity), retirement, divorce or death of spouse, new dependent, employment change with materially different compensation, or a client-initiated risk-tolerance change. A founder who has just exited (e.g., large cash proceeds sitting unallocated) must have an Investment Policy Statement drafted before any deployment beyond money-market sweeps.

## Senior and vulnerable client protocol

For clients aged 70+ or showing signs of diminished capacity:

- Encourage a trusted contact and, where appropriate, a power of attorney (POA) on file. Document the request annually if declined.
- Invite the trusted contact/POA holder to at least the annual review when health decline is observed. Note: observation of declining health belongs in the meeting note (facts only, no diagnosis language).
- Withdrawal-pattern anomalies (new payees, urgency, secrecy) must be escalated to the Senior Investor Protection desk within one business day.

## Concentration supervision

Any single equity position above 10% of household investable assets — regardless of origin (employer stock, inheritance, appreciation) — places the household on the monthly concentration report. The RM must present the Concentrated Stock Playbook options within 90 days of first flag and record the client's decision. "Client is emotionally attached to the position" is a documentation-worthy fact, not a resolution.

## Reviews and documentation

- Core segment: annual review minimum. Affluent: semi-annual. Private Wealth: quarterly.
- A review is overdue the day after its due date; overdue reviews appear on the RM's supervision dashboard and must be scheduled within 30 days.
- Every substantive client interaction gets a CRM note within 48 hours, including verbal instructions received and advice given.
