# Client Communication Policy

**Compliance manual chapter 7, revision 2026-05. Applies to email, letters, texts, and AI-drafted content.**

## Core rules

1. **No promissory language.** Never guarantee returns, income levels, or goal outcomes. Prohibited phrasing includes "will grow," "guaranteed income," "can't lose," "risk-free" (except for direct Treasury yield descriptions), and "assure." Preferred: "designed to," "historically has," "targets," "seeks to."
2. **Balanced presentation.** Any statement of potential benefit must be accompanied by the corresponding risk in the same communication. Performance figures require period, benchmark, and net-of-fee disclosure.
3. **No off-channel communication.** Business communication only through firm email, the client portal, or recorded firm telephony. WhatsApp/personal-text conversations must be moved to an approved channel and the prior thread uploaded to CRM.
4. **Forward-looking statements** must be framed as opinions of the investment office with the standard "subject to change; not a prediction" disclosure block.

## AI-generated drafts (chat copilot & workbench)

AI-drafted briefs, emails and commentary are **first drafts only**. The RM must:

- Verify all figures against the platform (AI outputs can be stale or wrong).
- Remove or soften any promissory phrasing before sending.
- Confirm product mentions are on-shelf and suitable for the recipient's risk profile.
- Retain the final sent version in CRM; the AI draft itself is not the record.

An AI draft sent verbatim without RM review is a supervisory violation attributable to the RM, not the tool.

## Specific disclosures

- **Municipal bonds:** income is federally tax-exempt; state taxation depends on the client's state of residence and the bonds held. Do not state "tax-free" without the federal/state qualifier.
- **Crypto assets:** must carry the digital-asset volatility disclosure and the statement that crypto is not FDIC/SIPC protected.
- **Market data in client materials:** cite the as-of timestamp; live dashboard quotes may be delayed and are not a trading basis.

## Complaint handling

Any written or verbal expression of dissatisfaction about handling of the account is a complaint. Forward to compliance the same business day; do not negotiate resolution directly. Acknowledge to the client within one business day using approved language.
