# Investment Policy Committee — Approved Product Shelf Notes

**IPC minutes extract, reviewed 2026-06-19. Applies to the 37-instrument retail shelf.**

## Shelf philosophy

The retail shelf is intentionally narrow: broad-market index ETFs from Vanguard, iShares/BlackRock, Schwab, Invesco and State Street, a curated set of large-cap single stocks, and BTC/ETH for eligible profiles. Anything off-shelf requires an IPC exception ticket before purchase.

## Core building blocks (default recommendations)

- **US equity:** VOO (S&P 500, 0.03% ER) or VTI (total market, 0.03% ER) as the primary sleeve. QQQ is a satellite, not a core — its Nasdaq-100 tech tilt double-counts risk for clients with tech employment or concentrated tech stock.
- **International:** VXUS (total international) preferred for single-fund simplicity; VEA + VWO acceptable where the RM wants to control the developed/emerging split.
- **Dividend/quality tilt:** SCHD (3.5% yield, 0.06% ER) and VIG for income-oriented or lower-volatility equity sleeves; both are approved for retirees.
- **Fixed income:** AGG or BND interchangeable as core; MUB for taxable accounts in high brackets; SHY/T-bill ladders for liabilities inside 3 years; TIP for inflation-linked sleeves; LQD for credit satellite.

## Watch-listed and restricted items

- **ARKK — WATCH status (since 2025-Q4).** High-volatility active fund, 0.75% ER. Max 5% of any portfolio; not eligible for Conservative or Moderately Conservative profiles. Existing overweight positions should be trimmed on strength, and suitability must be re-documented at each annual review.
- **TLT — tactical only.** Long-duration Treasuries carry ~17-year duration; a 1% rate rise implies roughly a 17% price decline. Positions must be tied to a documented rate thesis and sized under 5%. RMs must confirm the client understands duration risk (see Linda Okafor precedent, 2026-07 review note).
- **BTC / ETH.** Aggressive profiles: combined cap 5% of liquid net worth. Moderately Aggressive: 2%. Moderate and below: not eligible. Custody through the platform only — no self-custody wallets on advised assets. When appreciation pushes crypto above cap (e.g., a client at 15% of liquid net worth), the RM must present a written trim plan at the next contact, and document client refusal if declined.
- **Single stocks.** Approved list only (AAPL, MSFT, NVDA, AMZN, GOOGL, TSLA, JPM, JNJ, XOM, BRK-B, COST, UNH, V, PG, HD). Any single position above 10% of household investable assets triggers the concentration protocol regardless of how it arose.

## Expense-ratio guardrail

Portfolio-weighted average expense ratio should stay under 0.15% for Core segment clients and under 0.25% elsewhere, excluding the crypto sleeve. Justify any portfolio exceeding the guardrail in the IPS.
