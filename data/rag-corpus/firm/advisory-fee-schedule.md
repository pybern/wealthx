# WealthLens Advisory Fee Schedule

**Effective 2026-01-01. Version 4.2. Internal reference — always quote from the current client agreement.**

## Standard advisory fee (annual, billed quarterly in arrears)

| Assets under management | Fee rate |
|---|---|
| First $1,000,000 | 1.00% |
| $1,000,001 – $3,000,000 | 0.80% |
| $3,000,001 – $5,000,000 | 0.65% |
| $5,000,001 – $10,000,000 | 0.50% |
| Above $10,000,000 | 0.40% |

Fees are blended across tiers (marginal, like tax brackets). Example: a $5M household pays 1.00% on the first $1M, 0.80% on the next $2M, and 0.65% on the final $2M — a blended rate of approximately 0.78%, or about $39,000 per year.

## Segment definitions

- **Core:** under $1M in managed assets. Digital-first service model, annual review, pooled RM coverage.
- **Affluent:** $1M–$5M. Dedicated RM, semi-annual reviews.
- **Private Wealth:** above $5M or above $2M with complex planning needs (business sale, concentrated stock, trust structures). Dedicated RM plus planning specialist, quarterly reviews.

## What the fee includes

Portfolio management, rebalancing, tax-loss harvesting, financial planning, goal tracking, meeting preparation and RM access. Product-level expense ratios (e.g., 0.03% on VOO/VTI) are charged by fund issuers and are separate from the advisory fee — always disclose both when discussing costs.

## Discounts and exceptions

- Household aggregation: accounts of spouses and dependent children are combined for tier calculation.
- Employee of record at a client's firm (401(k) plan relationships): 10% courtesy discount, VP approval required.
- Legacy 2019-and-earlier agreements are grandfathered at their original schedule until the next material amendment.
- Fee exceptions beyond these require written approval from the regional director and must be logged in the exceptions register.

## Billing mechanics

Fees are calculated on the average daily balance of the quarter, deducted from the designated billing account within 10 business days of quarter end. Cash in the billing account below the projected fee triggers an RM task to raise cash or redirect billing. No performance fees are charged on any retail account.
