# CIO Capital Markets Outlook — Second Half 2026

**WealthLens Investment Office — published 2026-07-08. Internal use; language may be adapted for client communications after compliance review.**

## House view summary

We enter the second half of 2026 constructive but selective. Equity valuations are elevated versus the 10-year average, led by the AI-infrastructure complex, while the rate market is pricing two additional Fed cuts by year-end. Our base case (60% probability) is a soft-landing continuation: mid-single-digit earnings growth, inflation drifting toward 2.5%, and no recession before 2028.

## Asset-class views

- **US large-cap equity: Neutral.** Concentration at the index level is extreme — the top ten S&P 500 names are near 40% of index weight. We prefer adding through equal-weight or dividend-quality sleeves (SCHD, VIG) rather than cap-weighted top-ups for clients already tech-heavy.
- **US small-cap: Modest overweight.** Small caps (IWM) trade at the widest relative discount to large caps since 2001 and are the biggest beneficiary of rate cuts.
- **International developed: Overweight.** VEA-type exposure benefits from a softening dollar and cheaper multiples. Japan corporate-governance reform remains a multi-year tailwind.
- **Emerging markets: Neutral.** VWO exposure is appropriate only for Moderate-and-above risk profiles; China property-sector stress remains unresolved.
- **Fixed income: Extend duration selectively.** We recommend moving cash into the intermediate part of the curve (AGG, MUB for taxable clients) before the cutting cycle compresses reinvestment yields. Long duration (TLT) remains a tactical, not strategic, position — size it below 5% of portfolio.
- **Munis:** For clients in the 32%+ federal bracket, national munis (MUB) offer taxable-equivalent yields above 5%. New York and California residents should prefer in-state ladders where position size permits.
- **Gold (GLD): Hold.** Central-bank buying supports the floor; we do not chase strength above allocation targets.
- **Digital assets: Cap at 5% of liquid net worth** for Aggressive profiles, 2% for Moderately Aggressive, 0% below that. BTC and ETH only; no altcoins on the platform. Rebalance-trim when appreciation pushes the sleeve above target.

## Key risks we are watching

1. **AI capex digestion.** If hyperscaler capital expenditure guidance flattens, NVDA-led leadership could unwind quickly. Clients with single-position concentration above 10% should be moved through the Concentrated Stock Playbook now, not after a drawdown.
2. **Election-year fiscal noise** around the 2026 midterms; historically a volatility event, not a trend change.
3. **Commercial real estate refinancing wall** — we remain underweight office-heavy REITs within VNQ-style exposure.
