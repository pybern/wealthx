# RMD & Qualified Charitable Distribution (QCD) Procedure

**Advisory playbook 9, updated 2026-02. Applies to Traditional IRA, SEP, SIMPLE, and inherited retirement accounts.**

## RMD basics for 2026

- RMD age is 73 for clients born 1951–1959 (SECURE 2.0). First RMD may be delayed to April 1 of the following year, but that doubles income in one tax year — model both ways before recommending.
- The annual deadline is **December 31**. Firm operational cutoff: submit RMD distributions by **October 31** to avoid year-end processing risk; the supervision dashboard flags unscheduled RMDs on November 1.
- Penalty for a missed RMD is 25% of the shortfall (reduced to 10% if corrected within two years) — always file Form 5329 with a reasonable-cause letter if one slips.
- RMDs are calculated per-IRA but may be aggregated and taken from any one IRA. 401(k) RMDs cannot be aggregated with IRA RMDs.

## Qualified Charitable Distributions

A QCD sends IRA money directly to a public charity and **excludes it from taxable income** — better than taking the RMD and deducting the gift, because it works even with the standard deduction and keeps AGI down (which protects IRMAA Medicare brackets and taxation of Social Security).

- Eligibility begins at age **70½** (earlier than the RMD age).
- 2026 limit: **$108,000 per person** (indexed annually).
- The check must go **directly from custodian to charity** — if the client touches the money, it is a normal taxable distribution.
- QCDs count toward the RMD if executed before the RMD is otherwise satisfied. **Sequence matters:** process QCDs first, then the remaining RMD.
- Donor-advised funds and private foundations are **not** eligible QCD recipients; operating charities and qualifying charitable entities are. A client wanting to fund a hospital foundation directly is a textbook QCD case.

## RM workflow

1. January: RMD report generated per client; record planned strategy (cash raise, in-kind transfer, QCD split) in CRM.
2. Before processing: confirm charity's eligibility, exact legal name and mailing instructions in writing.
3. In-kind RMDs (moving shares out instead of cash) are allowed — useful when the client wants to keep the position; basis resets to distribution-date value in the taxable account.
4. Withholding: default federal withholding on RMDs is 10%; confirm the client's estimated-tax situation and set withholding intentionally, not by default.
5. Document completion and set the next-year reminder before closing the task (pattern: RMDs completed in November → calendar reminder created for October of the following year).
