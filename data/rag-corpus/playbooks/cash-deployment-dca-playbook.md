# Cash Deployment & Dollar-Cost-Averaging Playbook

**Advisory playbook 11, updated 2026-04. Trigger: uninvested cash above 5% of portfolio with no liability inside 12 months, or any post-liquidity-event balance.**

## Decision framework

The research is clear: lump-sum investing beats DCA about two-thirds of the time, because markets rise more often than they fall. But the behavioral reality is that clients frozen at all-time highs deploy **nothing** without a structure. The playbook goal is a signed schedule, not a debate about optimality.

Recommend by client state:

- **Comfortable, experienced investor:** lump sum, same week, into the IPS allocation.
- **Hesitant / first large sum / post-exit founder:** DCA over **6 months, equal monthly tranches**, with an acceleration clause (below) — this converts anxiety into a plan.
- **Extremely anxious or elderly:** up to 12 months, but never longer; beyond 12 months the drag cost dominates.

## Standard 6-month DCA template

1. Divide deployable cash into six equal tranches; invest on the first trading day of each month.
2. Park undeployed cash in SHY or a Treasury money-market sweep so it earns ~4% while waiting — never leave it in a 0.1% bank sweep.
3. **Acceleration clause:** if the S&P 500 closes 10% or more below its level at plan signing, deploy the next two tranches immediately. Buying the dip is pre-agreed, not a panic decision.
4. **No-postponement clause:** tranches execute regardless of headlines. The RM may not skip a tranche without a written client instruction (which becomes a CRM note and restarts the schedule discussion).
5. Rebalance into the IPS target at month 7.

## Post-liquidity-event sequencing (founder exits, business sales)

Before any market deployment: reserve taxes (often 25–35% of gross proceeds pending K-1/installment terms), fund 12 months of lifestyle cash, and complete the Investment Policy Statement. Only then start the DCA clock. Alternatives (private credit, venture secondaries, real estate) are deferred until the public-market core is fully deployed — the IPS should state this ordering explicitly so enthusiasm for alternatives doesn't jump the queue.

## Communicating the cost of waiting

Quantify cash drag in dollars, not percentages: $2.4M held at a 4% money-market rate versus a 7% balanced-portfolio expected return forgoes roughly $72,000 of expected value in a single year. Pair this with the acknowledgment that the schedule — not a market call — is what gets the client invested.
