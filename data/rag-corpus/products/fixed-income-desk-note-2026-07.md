# Fixed Income Desk Note — July 2026

**Product desk memo, 2026-07-15. For RM use in client conversations; not client-distributable without compliance review.**

## Curve and positioning

The 2s10s curve has re-steepened to +45bp as the front end prices two more Fed cuts into year-end 2026. Our recommended posture remains **intermediate-heavy**: the belly (3–7yr) captures most of the cut benefit without the volatility of the long end.

- **Core (AGG / BND):** duration ~6 years, yield ~4.4%. Still the default fixed-income sleeve. AGG and BND are interchangeable for allocation purposes and are each other's approved tax-loss-harvest pair.
- **Short (SHY):** ~1.9-year duration, ~4.2% yield. The correct parking spot for goal liabilities inside 3 years (home purchases, tuition, DCA cash awaiting deployment). Expect yield to grind lower as cuts land — do not present current yield as durable.
- **Long (TLT):** ~17-year duration. Up strongly on cut expectations, but a 50bp adverse move erases a year of carry. Reiterating IPC guidance: tactical only, max 5%, documented thesis. Clients holding TLT underwater from the 2025 rate-cut thesis should decide at the next review: hold to thesis with a defined exit level, or harvest the loss into AGG (approved pair after the 31-day window via BND).
- **Credit (LQD):** IG spreads at 92bp are tight by history; we are not adding. Carry is acceptable; upside is limited.
- **TIPS (TIP):** 10-year breakevens near 2.4%. Fairly priced insurance — hold at target for inflation-sensitive retirees, don't chase.

## Municipal strategy

MUB's tax-equivalent yield for a 37%-bracket client is approximately 5.1% — still the best risk-adjusted after-tax carry on the shelf for high earners' taxable accounts. Two refinements for Private Wealth:

1. **State-specific ladders** for NY and CA residents above $500k of muni allocation: in-state paper adds 60–90bp of tax-equivalent yield for top-bracket residents. Coordinate with the muni desk; the retail shelf's MUB is national-only.
2. **Ladder maintenance:** reinvest maturing rungs at the long end of the ladder while the curve is steepening; do not let maturities pile up in sweep cash.

## Retiree income guidance

For income-oriented households, the desk's model income stack is: SCHD dividends (~3.5%) + MUB/AGG coupon (~3.2–4.4%) + a 1–2 rung T-bill ladder for the current-year spending floor. This supports a ~4% household income draw without selling principal in flat markets. Frame it as "income from three pockets" — clients retain it better than allocation percentages.
